=== DRYCLEANER_COUNTER ===
NAME: Service Counter

A blue service counter where customers drop off and pick up their dry cleaning. The surface is clean but shows signs of constant use.

The counter has a small window where orders are passed through. Behind it, you can see the organized chaos of the dry cleaning operation.

=== GARMENT_CONVEYOR ===
NAME: Garment Conveyor

Clothes drift slowly along the rubber belt like sleepy commuters. Garment bags and folded shirts queue patiently for their turn at the machines.

The conveyor has been running so long it's developed a personality. It hums. It doesn't judge. It just carries things forward.

=== LOST_AND_FOUND ===
NAME: Lost & Found

A wooden bin overflowing with items that were never claimed. A single brown loafer. Something that might be a monocle. A tag that reads 'EST. 1987 - STILL WAITING'.

The bin has a certain dignity. These things have been here so long they've become part of the scenery. Someone's other glove. A cufflink. Stories without endings.

=== PRESSING_TABLE ===
NAME: Pressing Table

A large pressing table where garments are ironed and steamed. The surface is covered in a clean white fabric, ready for the next item.

This is where the final touches are put on cleaned garments - removing wrinkles and ensuring everything looks perfect before pickup.
