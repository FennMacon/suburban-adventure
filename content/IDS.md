# Flavor Content IDs

Reference for editing `content/flavor/*.txt`. Each ID appears in the game when the player inspects that object.

## Dry Cleaner (drycleaner.txt)
- `DRYCLEANER_COUNTER` – Service counter
- `GARMENT_CONVEYOR` – Garment conveyor belt
- `LOST_AND_FOUND` – Lost & found bin
- `PRESSING_TABLE` – Pressing table

## Grumby (Convenience Store) (grumby.txt)
- `GRUMBY_SHELF` – Store shelf
- `GRUMBY_FRIDGE` – Refrigerator
- `GRUMBY_ICE_CREAM` – Ice cream cooler
- `GRUMBY_MAGAZINE` – Magazine rack
- `GRUMBY_CANDY` – Candy display
- `GRUMBY_COFFEE_MACHINE` – Coffee machine
- `GRUMBY_COFFEE_CORNER` – Coffee corner (area under sign)

## Supermarket (supermarket.txt)
- `SUPERMARKET_SHELF` – Store shelf
- `SUPERMARKET_FRIDGE` – Drink fridge
- `SUPERMARKET_CHECKOUT` – Checkout counter
- `SUPERMARKET_ENDCAP` – Endcap display
- `SUPERMARKET_FLOOR_DISPLAY` – Floor display
- `SUPERMARKET_CANDY` – Candy display

## Donut Galaxy (donut.txt)
- `DONUT_COUNTER` – Coffee counter
- `DONUT_ESPRESSO` – Espresso machine
- `DONUT_CASE` – Donut display case
- `DONUT_REGISTER` – Cash register
- `DONUT_TIP_JAR` – Tip jar
- `DONUT_STRAW` – Straw dispenser
- `DONUT_RACK` – Donut rack
- `DONUT_MENU` – Menu board
- `DONUT_FLOATING` – Floating donut galaxy
- `DONUT_CONDIMENT` – Condiment station
- `DONUT_TRASH` – Trash bin
- `DONUT_RECYCLING` – Recycling bin

## Clothing Store (clothing.txt)

## Grohos (grohos.txt)

## Flower Shop (flowers.txt)
